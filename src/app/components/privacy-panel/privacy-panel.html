2048, gerçek hesaplar tutan bir oyundur. Verini nasıl işlediğimizi burada açıkça anlatıyoruz. İlke basit: toplamadığımız veri, korumamız gereken veri değildir — bu yüzden mümkün olduğunca az veri tutarız.

  • Anonim kullanım olayları: 90 gün.

2048 keeps real accounts. Here's plainly how we handle your data. The principle is simple: data we don't collect is data we don't have to protect — so we keep as little as possible.

What we collect and why

  • Username — identifies your account; shown in leaderboards and to friends. Required.
  • Password — stored only as an irreversible hash (PBKDF2); even we can't read your plaintext password.
  • Email — OPTIONAL. Your account works without it. Kept only for future account recovery; we don't email you or market to you.
  • Game progress — scores, level, gold, powers, achievements. For the game itself.
  • Friendships and private messages — for the social features.
  • Anonymous usage events — e.g. which mode is played (no identity/IP), to balance the game.

How long we keep it

  • Private messages: auto-deleted after 180 days.
  • Anonymous usage events: 90 days.
  • Resolved reports: 180 days; moderation notices: 1 year (appeal window).
  • Admin audit log: 1 year. Game rooms: a few hours.
  • Your account and progress: until you delete it. Expired data is auto-cleaned on the server.

Who can access it

You: download your data or delete your account anytime. Admins: for issues/reports, can see account info (not the password), scores, and the report/moderation history about you; can apply moderation (warn/mute/suspend) with a mandatory, logged reason. An admin cannot see your password and cannot log in as you.

Chat privacy

Your private messages are private. Admins cannot freely read your chats; there is no tool to pull arbitrary conversations. Only if someone reports you can just the reported message and a narrow window around it (at most ±3 messages) be reviewed — and that access is logged.

Download your data

Settings → "Download my data" exports everything tied to your account as a JSON file.

Delete your account — and what happens

Settings → "Delete account" (password-confirmed, permanent). Deleting fully removes: your account, game progress, scores (monthly/daily), friendships, blocks, messages you sent and received, moderation notices, and sessions. Your username becomes available again. This can't be undone.

Appeals

If you want to appeal a moderation action, reach us from your account email; an admin re-reviews it with the record.
